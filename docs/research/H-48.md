# H-48: a mapping that a conflicting second one takes, kept by nights the host keeps by a probe, on three networks

What H-48 asks of the engine, as [ADR-0257](../adr/0257-a-night-kept-by-the-hosts-probe.md) wrote it on 2026-11-14, before any run.
Its row, with its verdict and its ADRs, is in [the table of hypotheses](README.md).

## The hypothesis

[H-47](H-47.md) read that plain nights on A's patterns kept more of a mapping a conflicting pair takes than nights without replay in ten of twelve cells, and not in two. A host can fork an image, run both nights, and read which keeps more before it goes on. H-48 asks whether nights so kept keep A at least as well as nights without replay everywhere, and more in at least ten cells.

The runs are H-47's: under `Config::runs_a_settled_image()`, the slow-wave baseline at 1/2 and H-25's learning configuration. **On each of seeds 22, 23 and 24 at 1 024 units, from each of H-47's four starts, the pair runs to block 36 under gated nights, on H-47's paths 0, 5 and 6.** Then, in each of the twelve cells, a network and a start:
1. A's mean count at block 36 under gated nights is not below that under nights without replay by more than twice its paired SE;
2. in at least ten of the twelve cells, A's mean count at block 36 under gated nights exceeds that under nights without replay by more than twice its paired SE;
3. the pair's mean count at block 36 under gated nights is at least 56 of 64;
4. none of A's four couplings passes 1.30 of H-42's image's on its network, at any block's end of any stretch the gated chain runs, the forks the gate does not keep among them, or at any image read.

**Yes** when all four hold; otherwise **no**, with the network, the start and the clause.

**Predicted: yes.**

| Predicted reading | Bound |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. The cell where replay cost A | on seed 23's first mapping from the mirrored assignment, A's mean count at block 36 under gated nights over that under plain nights on A's patterns, positive |
| 2. What the gate gives up | A's mean count at block 36 under gated nights not below that under plain nights on A's patterns by more than twice its paired SE, in at least 8 of the 10 cells where H-47's clause 1 held |
| 3. The plain night kept | at more than 72 of the 144 gaps, twelve cells by three paths by four gaps |
| 4. The night without replay kept | on seed 24's eighth image from the assignment, at at least 7 of its 12 gaps |

A predicted reading that misses is recorded as a miss, and decides nothing.

## Its terms

Every term not defined here is [H-47's](H-47.md#its-terms): the starts, A, the pair, the checkpoints, a read, the paths, a mean count, the paired SE, the split and A's four couplings. The protocol ADR lists every constant of the harness with its value before any arm runs.
- **A gap**: after blocks 6, 12, 18 and 24 of the pair, H-47's.
- **The two forks of a gap**: from the image the pair's stretch wrote, H-47's night without replay and its plain night on A's patterns, each run as H-47's forms run it.
- **A fork's stretch**: the pair's next stretch from the fork's night image, as H-47's forms run it: 6 blocks after the gaps at blocks 6, 12 and 18, and 12 blocks to block 36 after the gap at block 24.
- **The probe**: each fork's image after its stretch, read on streams 16, 17 and 18 by H-47's read, one read of A and one of the pair on each, the reward withheld. A fork's score is the sum of its six reads' correct trials, of 384.
- **The choice**: the plain night's fork is kept when its score is at least the night without replay's; otherwise that fork is kept.
- **The gated chain**: the head, then at each gap the kept fork's night image and its stretch. Its image at a gap's checkpoint is the kept fork's night image, and at block 36 the kept fork's after the last stretch.
- **Gated nights**: the gated chain's form. Its reads are H-47's, sixteen of A and sixteen of the pair at each checkpoint, on streams 0 to 15, none of which the probe reads.
- **The comparisons**: nights without replay and plain nights on A's patterns are H-47's pinned rows of those forms on the same paths and streams, paired by path and stream as H-47 pairs its forms.
- **The cells where H-47's clause 1 held**: all but seed 23's first mapping from the mirrored assignment and seed 24's eighth image from the assignment.

## What the prediction rests on

- **Where replay kept more**: in the ten cells where H-47's plain night stood above the night without replay at block 36, it stood above at block 24 too. Right after the night, single replayed nights from the night without replay's chain stood above beyond 2 SE in eight, seven, seven and six of the twelve cells after blocks 6, 12, 18 and 24 (explorations 031 and 032).
- **Where it cost A, the cost came late**: on seed 23's first mapping from the mirrored assignment, the nights after blocks 6 and 12 alone read +1.9 ± 0.68 and +1.6 ± 0.55 right after them, while the four plain nights read −4.9 at block 36 and leaving any one out shrank the loss ([exploration 030](../explorations/030-one-cell-night-by-night.md)). The probe reads after the next stretch so that part of a late cost is in its reading.
- **On seed 24's eighth image from the assignment**, the nights after blocks 12, 18 and 24 alone read −7.3 ± 0.88, −12.1 ± 0.63 and −12.3 ± 0.58 right after them ([exploration 032](../explorations/032-each-later-nights-replay-alone-on-twelve-cells.md)). Its first plain night read +0.3 ± 0.64 at block 6 and, alone, −2.0 ± 0.88 at block 36 ([exploration 031](../explorations/031-the-first-nights-replay-on-twelve-cells.md)): if the gate keeps it, clause 1 is at risk in that cell.
- **Clause 2's margins**: two of H-47's ten cells held clause 1 by less than three paired SE: seed 23's eighth image from the assignment, +4.1 ± 1.45, where the night after block 12 alone read −1.9 ± 0.88 right after it; and seed 24's first mapping from the assignment, +3.8 ± 1.57. A night the gate rejects there may take a cell below the bound. Clause 2 allows two cells of twelve not above; seed 24's eighth image from the assignment, where the gate is expected to keep the night without replay at most gaps, is expected to be one of them.
- **The single nights ran from the night without replay's chain**, not from a gated one, so after the gate's first choice of the plain night no exploration has read the chain it runs.

## Its stopping rule

1. One round: brief 089 builds the gated night in the tests, with its ADR, then runs H-48 once. Its clauses, constants and readings are committed before any arm runs.
2. A calibration stops the round, and is a finding, if a pinned number moves. So does a gated chain that, where the gate has kept one form at every gap so far, is not H-47's pinned row of that form.
3. Yes: M11's exit test reads yes. The next decisions are whether M11 closes and whether the slow-wave baseline enters the running configuration.
4. No on clause 4: M12 reopens, and an ADR on the zero sum through the form that crossed.
5. No on clause 1: an ADR on what the probe after one stretch does not see on that network.
6. No on clause 2: an ADR on what the gate gives up where replay kept more.
7. No on clause 3: an ADR on what the gated nights cost the pair.
8. Where several steps apply, the first in this order is taken. After any verdict, an ADR orders ADR-0254's orders 4 to 7 beside M13, M14, M8's remainder and M15.
9. No constant moves after such a run; no second attempt, and no other probe, stream, tie rule, form, path or network.
