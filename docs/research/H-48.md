# H-48: a mapping that a conflicting second one takes, kept by nights the host keeps by a probe, on three networks

What H-48 asks of the engine, as [ADR-0257](../adr/0257-a-night-kept-by-the-hosts-probe.md) wrote it on 2026-11-14, before any run.
Its row, with its verdict and its ADRs, is in [the table of hypotheses](README.md).

## The hypothesis

[H-47](H-47.md) read that plain nights on A's patterns kept more of a mapping a conflicting pair takes than nights without replay in ten of twelve cells, and not in two. A host can fork an image, run both nights, and read which keeps more of A before it goes on. H-48 asks whether nights so kept keep A at least as well as nights without replay everywhere, and more in at least ten cells.

The runs are H-47's: under `Config::runs_a_settled_image()`, the slow-wave baseline at 1/2 and H-25's learning configuration. **On each of seeds 22, 23 and 24 at 1 024 units, from each of H-47's four starts, the pair runs to block 36 under gated nights, on H-47's paths 0, 5 and 6.** Then, over the twelve cells, a network and a start each:
1. in each cell, A's mean count at block 36 under gated nights is not below that under nights without replay by more than twice its paired SE;
2. in at least ten of the twelve cells, A's mean count at block 36 under gated nights exceeds that under nights without replay by more than twice its paired SE;
3. in each cell, the pair's mean count at block 36 under gated nights is at least 56 of 64;
4. in each cell, none of A's four couplings passes 1.30 of H-42's image's on its network, at any block's end of any stretch the gated chain runs, the forks the gate does not keep among them, or at any image read.

**Yes** when all four hold; otherwise **no**, with the network, the start and the clause.

**Predicted: no, on clause 2.**

| Predicted reading | Bound |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. Clause 1 | its rule holds in at least 11 of the 12 cells |
| 2. Clause 2 | its rule holds in at least 8 of the 12 cells |
| 3. Where replay cost A | on seed 23's first mapping from the mirrored assignment, A's mean count at block 36 under gated nights over that under plain nights on A's patterns, positive |
| 4. The gate's choices where replay cost A late | on seed 24's eighth image from the assignment, the plain night kept at the gap at block 6 on at least two of its three paths, and the night without replay at at least seven of its nine later gaps |

A predicted reading that misses is recorded as a miss, and decides nothing.

## Its terms

Every term not defined here is [H-47's](H-47.md#its-terms): the starts, A, the pair, the checkpoints, a read, the paths, a mean count, the paired SE, the split and A's four couplings. The protocol ADR lists every constant of the harness with its value before any arm runs.
- **A gap**: after blocks 6, 12, 18 and 24 of the pair, H-47's.
- **The two forks of a gap**: from the image the pair's stretch wrote, H-47's night without replay and its plain night on A's patterns, each run as H-47's forms run it.
- **Six blocks on**: each fork runs the pair's next 6 blocks from its night image, as H-47's stretches run, from the path's first index.
- **The probe**: each fork's image six blocks on, read on streams 16 to 21 by H-47's read, one read of A on each, the reward withheld. A fork's score is the sum of its six reads' correct trials, of 384.
- **The choice**: the plain night's fork is kept when its score is at least the night without replay's; otherwise that fork is kept.
- **The gated chain**: the head, then at each gap the kept fork's night image and, after it, the pair's blocks to the next gap. After the gaps at blocks 6, 12 and 18 those are the kept fork's six blocks; after the gap at block 24 they are H-47's 12 blocks to block 36, from the kept fork's night image.
- **Gated nights**: the gated chain's form. Its reads are H-47's, sixteen of A and sixteen of the pair at each checkpoint, on streams 0 to 15. The probe reads none of those images and none of those streams.
- **The comparisons**: nights without replay and plain nights on A's patterns are H-47's pinned rows of those forms on the same paths and streams, paired by path and stream as H-47 pairs its forms.

## What the prediction rests on

Explorations 030 to 032 read single replayed nights, each from the night without replay's chain, and forms leaving one plain night out. They are exploratory, not evidence; no exploration has read a chain a gate runs.
- **Six blocks on**, at the first checkpoint after the probe would read, the form that replays on one night alone stood above the night without replay beyond 2 SE in 5, 9, 7 and 3 of the twelve cells after the nights at blocks 6, 12, 18 and 24, and below in 1, 2, 1 and 2 ([exploration 031](../explorations/031-the-first-nights-replay-on-twelve-cells.md), [exploration 032](../explorations/032-each-later-nights-replay-alone-on-twelve-cells.md)).
- **Seed 24's eighth image from the assignment**: the first plain night read +1.4 ± 0.72 at block 12, and the later single nights −4.6 ± 1.83, −8.8 ± 0.82 and −4.6 ± 0.73 at blocks 18, 24 and 36. The form that replays on the first night alone stood +6.4 ± 1.70 over the plain nights at block 18, and −2.0 ± 0.88 under the night without replay at block 36. The gate is expected to run that form: not above there, and clause 1 at risk.
- **Seed 23's eighth image from the assignment**: the night after block 12 alone read −2.3 ± 0.76 at block 18, and every form that replays on one night alone stood within or below 2 SE at block 36, +1.3 ± 1.04 to −5.6 ± 1.92. Not above there.
- **Seed 23's first mapping from the mirrored assignment**: the night after block 24 alone read −2.4 ± 0.86 at block 36. The form leaving that night out read −1.3 ± 1.40 there, within 2 SE, and every form leaving a night out stood above the plain nights ([exploration 030](../explorations/030-one-cell-night-by-night.md)). Not above there, and above the plain nights.
- **Those three cells** put clause 2 one below its bound. Two other cells held H-47's clause 1 by less than three paired SE, seed 23's eighth image and seed 24's first mapping from the assignment, so a rejected night there could take a fourth below.
- **The probe's resolution**: exploration 017 split a read's SD of 3.52 into a stream's 2.64 and the rest's 2.34. Two forks read on the same six streams share the streams' part. Worked out and not run, their difference over six reads has an SD of at most √12 × 2.34 ≈ 8.1 correct trials, 1.35 a read, so a true difference under about 1.5 a read is chosen by chance on some paths.

## Its stopping rule

1. One round: brief 089 builds the gated night in the tests and writes its protocol ADR, then runs H-48 once. Its clauses, constants and readings are committed before any arm runs.
2. A calibration stops the round, and is a finding, if a pinned number moves. So does the calibration test, run beside the jobs, if a fork's image or reads at block 6 on seed 22's eighth image from the assignment, path 0, are not H-47's pinned row of its form. So does a gated chain that, where the gate has kept the same form at every gap so far, is not H-47's pinned row of that form.
3. Yes: M11's exit test reads yes. The next decisions are whether M11 closes, whether the slow-wave baseline enters the running configuration, and whether DR-25 runs.
4. No on clause 4: M12 reopens, and an ADR on the zero sum through the form that crossed.
5. No on clause 1: an ADR on what the probe six blocks on does not see on that network.
6. No on clause 2: an ADR on what the gate gives up where replay kept more.
7. No on clause 3: an ADR on what the gated nights cost the pair.
8. Where several steps apply, the first in this order is taken. After any verdict an ADR sets when ADR-0254's order 2 runs, and orders its orders 4 to 7 beside M13, M14, M8's remainder and M15.
9. No constant moves after such a run; no second attempt, and no other probe, stream, tie rule, form, path or network.
