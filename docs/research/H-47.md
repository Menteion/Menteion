# H-47: a mapping that a conflicting second one takes, more of it kept by nights with replay than by nights without, on three networks

## The hypothesis

On seed 22, explorations read that a mapping does not fade under the drive. A second mapping sharing 26 of its 51 source units and conflicting with it took it by the share. These are exploratory readings, not evidence. M11 asks whether a sleep with replay keeps more of it than a sleep without.

The runs are under `Config::runs_a_settled_image()`, with the slow-wave baseline at 1/2 and H-25's learning configuration. **On each of seeds 22, 23 and 24 at 1 024 units, from each of the four starts, the pair runs to block 36 under each of the four forms, on paths 0, 5 and 6.** Then, in each of the twelve cells, a network and a start:
1. A's mean count at block 36 under plain nights on A's patterns exceeds that under nights without replay by more than twice its paired SE;
2. the pair's mean count at block 36 under plain nights on A's patterns is at least 56 of 64;
3. under every form, none of A's four couplings passes 1.30 of H-42's image's on its network, at any block's end of the pair's 36 or at any image read.

**Yes** when all three hold; otherwise **no**, with the network, the start and the clause.

**Predicted: yes.**

## Its terms

Each term is exploration 018's or 019's, on the tag `explore/019-a-night-without-replay`. The protocol ADR lists every constant of the harness with its value before any arm runs.
- **The starts**: H-46's eighth image in each of H-25's two arms, and a first mapping learned over 24 blocks from H-42's image in each arm. **A** is the mapping in force there, its two stimuli A and B.
- **The pair**: a second mapping whose stimulus C shares 26 of A's 51 source units and D 26 of B's 51, its answers conflicting with A's. The zero sum holds six groups, each kind of unit apart at its sum in the start image: A alone, A's and C's, C alone, and the same of B and D.
- **The gaps**: after blocks 6, 12, 18 and 24 of the pair, then 12 blocks with none to block 36.
- **The four forms of a gap.** A **plain night on A's patterns** starts in slow-wave sleep at shift 7 and a pressure of 1.0, under the drive, the zero sum's groups held, every older episode spent. It replays A's patterns, drawn from the start image, tagged at priority 200. A **night without replay** is that night with nothing tagged. A **plain night on both** also replays the pair's patterns, drawn from the image the night follows. The **awake gap** is 4 037 214 idle ticks under the drive.
- **The checkpoints**: right after each gap, at blocks 6, 12, 18 and 24, and at block 36's end.
- **A read**: one block of 64 trials on a fork of the image, the reward withheld. An image is read sixteen times, as it was and fifteen times by `Both`, exploration 017's `ReadFrom`.
- **The paths**: 0, 5 and 6, exploration 017's, each its own run of the pair from the start.
- **A mean count**: of `correct` over a cell's 48 reads of one form at one checkpoint, sixteen on each path's image.
- **The paired SE**: on each path the two forms' means of sixteen differ, and the difference is the mean of those three. Its SE is $\sqrt{s^2/16 + d^2/3}$. Here $s$ is the stream's SD in the split of the 48 differences, a negative variance as zero, and $d$ the SD of the three paths' differences.
- **The split**: a read, a path, a stream and the rest, as explorations 017 and 018 define them. The protocol ADR restates it.
- **A's four couplings**: A's and B's units onto the two readouts, each over the same coupling in H-42's image on that network.
- **A's margin**: $c_0 + c_3 - c_1 - c_2$ of A's four couplings, negated when the read is mirrored, the mean over the three paths, in millions (M).
