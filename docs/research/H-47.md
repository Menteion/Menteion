# H-47: a mapping that a conflicting second one takes, kept by nights with replay against nights without, on three networks

What H-47 asks of the engine, as [ADR-0234](../adr/0234-learning-that-sleep-keeps.md) wrote it on 2026-11-02, before any run.
Its row, with its verdict and its ADRs, is in [the table of hypotheses](README.md).

## The hypothesis

A mapping does not fade under the drive, and a second mapping that shares half of its source units and conflicts with it takes it by the share. M11 asks whether a sleep with replay keeps it, against a sleep without.

Under `Config::runs_a_settled_image()` with its groups set, the slow-wave baseline at 1/2 and H-25's learning configuration:

**On each of seeds 22, 23 and 24 at 1 024 units, from each of four starts (H-46's eighth image in each of H-25's two arms, and a first mapping learned from H-42's image in each arm), A being the mapping in force there:** a second mapping, the pair, whose stimuli share 26 of A's 51 source units and conflict with A, runs 24 blocks with a gap at blocks 6, 12, 18 and 24, then 12 blocks with none, on paths 0, 5 and 6. Each gap is a night at shift 7, the drive on, the groups held, every older episode spent. Then, in each of the twelve cells, a network and a start:
1. A's mean count at block 36 after plain nights on A's patterns exceeds that after nights without replay by more than twice its paired SE;
2. the pair's mean count at block 36 after plain nights on A's patterns is at least 56 of 64;
3. under plain nights on A's patterns, none of A's four couplings passes 1.30 of H-42's image's on its network, at any block's end or image read.

**Yes** when all three hold; otherwise **no**, with the network, the start and the clause.

**A mean count** is of `correct` over an image's 48 reads: three paths by sixteen, the read as it was and fifteen by `Both`, each on a fork of the image. **The paired SE**: on each path the two forms' means of sixteen differ, and the difference is their mean over the paths. Its SE is $\sqrt{s^2/16 + d^2/3}$, where $s$ is the stream's SD in the split of the 48 differences, a negative variance as zero, and $d$ the SD of the paths' differences.

**Predicted: yes.**

| Predicted reading | Bound |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. A over the awake gap | A's mean count at block 36 after plain nights on A's patterns over the awake gap's, positive in at least 10 of the 12 cells |
| 2. Both replayed | A's mean count at block 36 after plain nights on both over that after nights without replay, positive in every cell |
| 3. A's margin | at block 36 after plain nights on A's patterns, at least 2 M over that after nights without replay, in every cell |
| 4. The gain | every night without replay raises it, and every plain night lowers it |
| 5. Right after the last night | clause 1's difference at block 24 above twice its paired SE in at least 10 of the 12 cells |

A predicted reading that misses is recorded as a miss, and decides nothing.

## Its stopping rule

1. One round: brief 087 builds the slow-wave baseline, the ledger spent and the trial's own units with their tests and ADR, then runs H-47 once. Its clauses, constants and readings are committed before any arm runs.
2. A calibration stops the round, and is a finding, if a pinned number moves with the three parts unset. So does a twin of exploration 019 on seed 22 that is not its log: the eighth image from the assignment, path 0, the head and the first night without replay with its reads.
3. Yes: M11's exit test reads yes. The next decision is whether the slow-wave baseline enters the running configuration, and whether M11 closes.
4. No on clause 3: M12 reopens, and an ADR on the zero sum through a night.
5. No on clause 1: an ADR on what keeps A on that network, the replay or the rest of the night.
6. No on clause 2: an ADR on what a replay of A costs the pair.
7. Where several steps apply, the first in this order is taken.
8. No constant moves after such a run; no second attempt, and no other baseline, shift, form, path or network.
