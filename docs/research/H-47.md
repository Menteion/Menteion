# H-47: a mapping that a conflicting second one takes, more of it kept by nights with replay than by nights without, on three networks

What H-47 asks of the engine, as [ADR-0234](../adr/0234-learning-that-sleep-keeps.md) wrote it on 2026-11-02, before any run.
Its row, with its verdict and its ADRs, is in [the table of hypotheses](README.md).

## The hypothesis

On seed 22, explorations read that a mapping does not fade under the drive. A second mapping sharing 26 of its 51 source units and conflicting with it took it by the share. These are exploratory readings, not evidence. M11 asks whether a sleep with replay keeps more of it than a sleep without.

Under `Config::runs_a_settled_image()`, the slow-wave baseline at 1/2 and H-25's learning configuration, **on each of seeds 22, 23 and 24 at 1 024 units, from each of the four starts, the pair runs through its four gaps to block 36 on paths 0, 5 and 6, under each of the four forms.** Then, in each of the twelve cells, a network and a start:
1. A's mean count at block 36 under plain nights on A's patterns exceeds that under nights without replay by more than twice its paired SE;
2. the pair's mean count at block 36 under plain nights on A's patterns is at least 56 of 64;
3. under every form, none of A's four couplings passes 1.30 of H-42's image's on its network, at any block's end of the pair's 36 or at any image read.

**Yes** when all three hold; otherwise **no**, with the network, the start and the clause.

**Predicted: yes.**

| Predicted reading | Bound |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1. A over the awake gap | A's mean count at block 36 under plain nights on A's patterns over that under the awake gap, positive in at least 10 of the 12 cells |
| 2. Both replayed | A's mean count at block 36 under plain nights on both over that under nights without replay, positive in every cell |
| 3. A's margin | at block 36 under plain nights on A's patterns, at least 2 M over that under nights without replay, in every cell |
| 4. The gain | from its `start` line to its `end` line, every night without replay raises it, and every plain night, on A's patterns or on both, lowers it |
| 5. Right after the last night | clause 1's difference at block 24 above twice its paired SE in at least 10 of the 12 cells |

A predicted reading that misses is recorded as a miss, and decides nothing.

## Its terms

Each term is exploration 018's or 019's, on the tag `explore/019-a-night-without-replay`. The protocol ADR lists every constant of the harness with its value before any arm runs.
- **The starts**: H-46's eighth image in each of H-25's two arms, and a first mapping learned over 24 blocks from H-42's image in each arm. **A** is the mapping in force there, its two stimuli A and B.
- **The pair**: a second mapping whose stimuli C and D share 26 of A's and B's 51 source units, and conflict with A's answers. The zero sum holds six groups, each kind of unit apart at its sum in the start image: A alone, A's and C's, C alone, and the same of B and D.
- **The gaps**: after blocks 6, 12, 18 and 24 of the pair, then 12 blocks with none to block 36.
- **The four forms of a gap.** A **plain night on A's patterns** starts in slow-wave sleep at shift 7 and a pressure of 1.0, under the drive, every older episode spent. It replays A's patterns, drawn from the start image, tagged at priority 200. A **night without replay** is that night with nothing tagged. A **plain night on both** also replays the pair's patterns, drawn from the image the night follows. The **awake gap** is 4 037 214 idle ticks under the drive.
- **The checkpoints**: right after each gap, at blocks 6, 12, 18 and 24, and at block 36's end.
- **A read**: one block of 64 trials on a fork of the image, the reward withheld. An image is read sixteen times, as it was and fifteen times by `Both`, exploration 017's `ReadFrom`.
- **The paths**: 0, 5 and 6, exploration 017's, each its own run of the pair from the start.
- **A mean count**: of `correct` over a cell's 48 reads of one form at one checkpoint, sixteen on each path's image.
- **The paired SE**: on each path the two forms' means of sixteen differ, and the difference is the mean of those three. Its SE is $\sqrt{s^2/16 + d^2/3}$. Here $s$ is the stream's SD in the split of the 48 differences, a negative variance as zero, and $d$ the SD of the three paths' differences.
- **The split**: a read, a path, a stream and the rest, as explorations 017 and 018 define them.
- **A's four couplings**: A's and B's units onto the two readouts, each over the same coupling in H-42's image on that network.
- **A's margin**: $c_0 + c_3 - c_1 - c_2$ of A's four couplings, negated when the read is mirrored, the mean over the three paths, in M.

## What the prediction rests on

- **Seed 22's four cells** are expected to reproduce explorations 018 and 019, so the prediction rests on the eight cells of seeds 23 and 24, which no run has read.
- **Clause 1's smallest margin on seed 22** was +2.2 ± 0.6. Under 018's unpaired SE it was ± 1.1, within 2 SE, and the clause asks for all twelve cells.
